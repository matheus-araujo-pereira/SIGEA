\begin{resumo}[Abstract]
\begin{otherlanguage*}{english}
This document consolidates the formal Prior Art and Technological Forecasting Report conducted for the conceptual design, intellectual property protection, and validation of the SIGEA-GTT system (Intelligent Adverse Event Management System -- Global Trigger Tool). The platform was conceived as an innovative software solution for active identification, systematic tracking, and audit of clinical risks grounded in the Global Trigger Tool (GTT) methodology of the Institute for Healthcare Improvement (IHI), featuring as its core inventive differentiator a synchronous, non-punitive pedagogical application for undergraduate Nursing and Medical education. The state-of-the-art search was executed on September 2, 2026 across five premier national and international databases of patents and peer-reviewed scientific literature: WIPO Patentscope, Scopus, Web of Science, SciELO, and the CAPES Journal Portal, structured through a tripartite boolean query strategy (Patient Safety, Software Engineering, and Education/Training). In WIPO Patentscope, refinement under IPC class G16H yielded 86 correlated documents, from which four patent applications stand out as the closest prior art (CN118888101, CN111627533, CN113379384, and US20210074398), all strictly directed toward corporate hospital billing, administrative liability, or static curricular lecturing. In scientific literature, three studies outline the research frontier regarding interdisciplinary validation, psychometric usability evaluation, and mitigation of the ``second victim'' psychological phenomenon. Technical analysis confirms that SIGEA-GTT fully satisfies the legal requirements of Novelty, Inventive Step, and Industrial Applicability as established by the Brazilian Industrial Property Law (Law No. 9,279/1996), supporting the technological roadmap toward filing a patent application and software copyright registration before the National Institute of Industrial Property (INPI).

\textbf{Keywords}: Prior Art Search. Technological Forecasting. Patents. Global Trigger Tool. Adverse Events. Patient Safety. Software Engineering. SIGEA-GTT.
\end{otherlanguage*}
\end{resumo}
